## 0.7.3

- **Fixed: modules you bought or spawned were secretly stuck at Level 1.** Only *crafted* modules\
  ever had a real level. A module bought from a trader — or taken from the creative menu, where it\
  showed a `*` instead of a number — came out with no level at all and behaved as Level 1: the\
  shortest reach and the slowest actions, no matter what you paid or which trader you bought from.\
  Buying a module is worth it again.

- **Modules now show up in the creative menu**, at a real Level 1–6. They were missing from it\
  entirely before. Tip: type `#6` in the creative search box to force everything it shows to Level 6.

- **Modules now have an effects tab.** The item window gains a second tab next to the description\
  showing what the level actually buys you — working range and action speed as a percentage of that\
  module's maximum. Overclock and the Wide-Band Antenna show the boost they apply to every other\
  module instead. The numbers come from the real scaling curve, so they stay accurate even if you\
  retune `droneautomation.xml`.

- **Verified on 7 Days to Die 3.1**, as well as 3.0.
